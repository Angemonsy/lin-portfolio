Logistics & Compliance

The business only works if material claims, delivery promises, and after-sales rules are clear.

Jewelry cross-border commerce has attractive margins, but trust can collapse quickly. The site therefore turns compliance into product experience, not a hidden footnote.

Material Claims

  • Use precise terms: 925 sterling silver, gold vermeil, plated brass, or solid gold.
  • Do not mix plated, filled, vermeil, and solid gold in product copy.
  • Keep supplier batch records and sample inspection notes.

Cross-border Delivery

  • D2C parcels use tracked small-packet or dedicated line logistics.
  • B2B orders use sampled production, balance payment, export declaration, then freight delivery.
  • Shipping pages disclose customs, tax responsibility, and estimated delivery windows.

Market Risk

  • US pages follow FTC Jewelry Guides for truthful jewelry advertising.
  • EU expansion requires attention to REACH restrictions such as nickel, lead, and cadmium.
  • UK sales may trigger hallmarking rules depending on metal and weight.
